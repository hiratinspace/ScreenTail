Callouts

  1. Title bar: the ticket summary, client, duration and start time come from the draft. The Published badge appears after publish. Discard is ghost-styled and asks for a typed confirmation. Publish ▸ mirrors the right pane.
  2. Note: fixed sections Problem → Steps → Result → Follow-ups. Everything is inline-editable, and "Saved / Saving…" shows bottom-left. Banners stack at the top: partial capture, frames purged, local draft, offline.
  3. Steps: the drag handle appears on hover. Alt+↑/↓ reorders; Enter at the end adds a step; Backspace on an empty step deletes it. Frame chips highlight their screenshot on hover and open it on click. Transcript snippets are in monospace.
  4. Low confidence: ⚠ with the tooltip "Inferred from screen only — please verify". Editing the step or pressing Alt+C clears it. In the wireframe a black left bar stands in for the confidence.low colour.
  5. Screenshots: 160 × 90 thumbnails in time order. Excluded ones are dimmed. Striped rows are the suppressed and out-of-scope gaps. Blur is destructive, with one-step undo until the pane loses focus. Delete gives a 5 s undo toast, then it's permanent.
  6. Publish: the searchable ticket picker (#id · summary · company) shows recent tickets on focus and the Suggested badge when the ticket was inferred. When Publish is disabled, the reason appears underneath as well as in the tooltip.
  7. Note type default: Internal is selected here; that's open question Q2 in README.md.
  8. Timeline: collapsed to a 40 px scrubber, 220 px when open. Dots are clicks, squares are frames, ⚑ is a marked moment, and hatched bands are suppression and scope. Clicking a transcript line highlights its frame. Redacted speech shows [REDACTED].

Keyboard

Every shortcut in Spec §5 S3 works here. Press ? for the sheet. Click a screenshot, then use Space B Del Enter.

Edge states (state bar above)

  • Draft failed: the note pane shows Retry; screenshots and the timeline stay usable. Publish is disabled (see open question Q4).
  • Offline: the "Draft pending — offline" banner, and Publish disabled with a reason.
  • No PSA connected: Publish is replaced by "Connect a PSA to publish".
  • Partial publish failure: the note that went through stays; the failed time entry gets Retry; the toast follows Spec §6 wording.
