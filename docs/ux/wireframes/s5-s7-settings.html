Delete everything on this device?

Deletes every session, draft, screenshot and transcript, your settings, and the sign-in tokens for ConnectWise and Hudu. You'll need to set ScreenTail up again. It can't be undone.

Notes

  1. Scope: "All windows" shows an inline warning and needs a confirming click before it saves (INV-5). Try selecting it.
  2. Hotkeys: conflicts show inline with a suggested alternative (ST-029). No dialogs.
  3. Data flow: the plain-language diagram must match the security data-flow doc (ST-114).
  4. Custom regex: validated as you type, with a test box. An invalid pattern blocks saving and says why (ST-081). Try typing (.
  5. Test connection: errors follow the Spec §4 pattern: what happened, then what to do. Secrets are masked after saving, and "Change" reveals the field. Click Test to see the error case.
  6. Company mapping: auto-matched with a confidence badge, and editable.
