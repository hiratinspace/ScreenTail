ScreenTail writes your ticket notes

It watches your remote sessions, then drafts the note for you: problem, steps, result and follow-ups.

Nothing is published without your review.

Setup takes about 5 minutes.

Your remote tools

We found these on this computer. Capture starts when one of them has focus.

What gets captured: only remote-session windows and admin tools such as PowerShell and Event Viewer. Your email, chat and other apps are never captured. You can change this later in Settings → Capture.

Notes

  • Seven steps, 5 minutes at most; progress dots at the top. Back works everywhere except step 1.
  • Step 3: every check shows ✓ or a fix. A blocked microphone deep-links to Windows settings and offers "Check again" (ST-083).
  • Step 4: detected tools are pre-checked, and the scope explanation states the recommended default in plain words (INV-5).
  • Step 5 is skippable: drafts are still created, and Review shows "Connect a PSA to publish" (S3 state).
  • Step 6 builds trust: the technician sees the pill, then a real draft from their own 60 seconds.
