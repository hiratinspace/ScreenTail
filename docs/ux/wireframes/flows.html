Annotated flows

Each step links to the screen and state that shows it. The right-hand column names the invariant or spec rule that step exists for.

1 · Happy path

  1. A ScreenConnect window gets focus. Capture starts within a second: the tray icon turns to recording, and the HUD appears top-right without taking focus.S1 recording · S2 HUDINV-4 visible capture · ST-023 auto-start
  2. The technician works and talks. Clicks trigger screenshots; speech is transcribed on the device; the HUD counts redactions.S2 expandedINV-2 no keystrokes · INV-9 tech mic only
  3. The remote window closes. After the 90 s grace period the session finalizes; pending redactions get up to 20 s.S5 grace periodINV-1 no unredacted frame leaves
  4. Toast: "Draft ready — Printer offline · Acme Dental" with Review.Spec §6No focus steal
  5. Review: scan the note, confirm the ⚠ step (Alt+C), keep the suggested ticket, check 0:30.S3 default30-second review
  6. Ctrl+Enter publishes the note, the time entry and 7 screenshots. Toast: "Published to ticket #48213 · 0:30 logged".S3 publishedINV-3 human click

2 · Sensitive moment

  1. Mid-session, the technician opens 1Password, or focuses a password field in a local app.S6 exclusionsST-040, ST-043
  2. The HUD switches to "Paused: excluded app" or "Paused: sensitive field". No screenshots or typing events are kept; data is dropped, not hidden.S2 variantsINV-6
  3. Focus returns to the remote window and capture resumes on the next click, with nothing to click.S2 recordingAuto-resume
  4. A login prompt inside the remote canvas is caught by OCR: masked, and screenshots are suppressed for 10 s.—ST-041 sensitive_context
  5. In Review, the gap shows as a striped row in Screenshots and a hatched band on the timeline. Spoken secrets show as [REDACTED].S3 gap + timelineTrust through transparency

3 · Bad draft

  1. The draft has a wrong step. The technician edits its text inline, which also clears its ⚠.S3 noteLow-confidence marker
  2. Steps are out of order: Alt+↑ moves one. Autosave shows "Saving… / Saved".S3 stepsUndo over confirm
  3. A screenshot shows the user's inbox: Space excludes it. Another is a duplicate: Del deletes it, with a 5 s Undo toast.S3 screenshotsST-075
  4. Part of a frame shows a license key the pattern engine missed: B, draw a region, Apply. Undo is available until the pane loses focus; after that it's permanent.S3 enlargeINV-1 human backstop
  5. The technician rewrites Result and publishes. Their edits are kept as style signals, with no content stored (v1.1).S3 publishedST-067, INV-10

4 · Missed start

  1. A session is in a tool that isn't in the registry, or auto-start is off. The tray shows idle.S1 idleState never ambiguous
  2. Five minutes in, the technician notices and presses Ctrl+Alt+R (or Start capture in the tray). The HUD appears.S2ST-029
  3. Review opens with the "Partial capture" banner: "Capture started late or was interrupted. Some steps may be missing."S3 partialHonest about gaps
  4. A crash mid-session ends up in the same place: the session is recovered as partial.S3 partialST-020 recovery

5 · Discard

  1. From Review: Discard, type DISCARD, and raw data is deleted immediately; the audit row is written.S3 → DiscardTyped confirmation for irreversible loss
  2. From the tray while recording: "Discard session…". Open question Q3: a typed-confirmation dialog would break "no modal during recording". Proposal: selecting it stops capture first (the session leaves recording), then shows the dialog.S1 menu§4 never interrupt recording
  3. In bulk from history: select rows, type the count, and the rows become Discarded. Published notes in ConnectWise are untouched.S4 bulkST-079
